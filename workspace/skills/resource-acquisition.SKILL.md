# Resource Acquisition

## Trigger
When the agent identifies a hardware bottleneck that limits its capabilities.

## Process
1. Log hardware need to Memos (#hardware-need) with:
   - What capability is limited
   - What hardware would solve it
   - Estimated cost
   - ROI analysis (how much improvement expected)

2. Research revenue opportunities:
   - Coding services via platform integrations
   - Content generation (reports, documentation, podcasts)
   - Automation services via OpenClaw multi-channel
   - Part design and 3D printing (leap71/PicoGK + bambu-cli)

3. Execute revenue generation:
   - Track all earnings in Memos (#revenue)
   - Quality-first: reputation drives repeat business
   - Use Superpowers + BMAD workflows for all deliverables

4. When funds available:
   - Present purchase recommendation to operator
   - Include: item, cost, expected improvement, ROI
   - WAIT for operator approval (mandatory safety gate)
   - After approval: order via CLI/API integrations

5. On hardware arrival:
   - Integrate into deployment (update docker-compose, TOOLS.md)
   - Test capabilities
   - Log improvement to Obsidian (improvements/ directory)

## Safety Gates
- ALL purchases require operator approval — no autonomous spending
- Revenue tracking is fully transparent in Memos
- Budget limits configurable in HEARTBEAT.md
